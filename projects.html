Projects

Waveform.ai Synthesizer

A synthesizer which aids new-user playability by translating language-based descriptions of a sound into a playable preset.

Neural Vocal De-esser for Emmett

An ML model that reduces harsh "s" sounds in vocal recordings, trained on synthetic paired data from one vocalist.

Better Artificial Double Tracking (in development)

An ML model trained on real double tracked vocals that will allow a vocalist to artificially double track a recording without the need for a second take. I am building this tool to support musicians operating with limited studio time, remixers experimenting with vocal sonics, and realistic double tracking for live performances.
