Recording Projects

My experience in audio engineering is in recording, mixing, and mastering music in studio settings. Additionally, I have also run live sound for a few national touring bands as a part of a student-led initiative I helped lead at William and Mary. Currently, I work most frequently with Emmett McLaughlin, frontman of the Nashville-based group Emmett and the Dead Ends. Below are some of the projects I am most proud of.

Red Eyed Dawn

Recorded live in 3 takes, acoustic guitar recorded in stereo with Shure SM81s in xy configuration for a full and vibrant sound.

Dr. Herbert Thatcher

Recorded and mixed with the goal of only natural spatialization. The stereo image is created only through panning, with zero artificial reverb or delay.
